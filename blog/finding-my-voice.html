Why I'm Finding My Voice Again

By Kirti Kaushal · July 2026 · 3 min read

A few people have asked me why I'm not as vocal about women's issues as I was 12–13 years ago.

The honest answer is that life happened.

Life Happened

Marriage, responsibilities, a full-time corporate career, household work, and trying to meet everyone's expectations slowly pushed my own voice into the background. Like many women, I spent years taking care of everything around me while quietly setting aside my own dreams.

There were moments when I questioned myself. Despite knowing my work, I often lacked the confidence to speak up in rooms where I knew I belonged. I wasn't less capable — I had simply become exhausted.

What Never Changed

But one thing never changed: my purpose.

For more than a decade, I've wanted to contribute to a world where women feel safe, respected, confident, and financially independent. I believe every woman deserves the opportunity to learn, build, earn, and make her own choices.

Starting Again

Today, I'm choosing to start again.

I'm building in public — not because I have all the answers, but because I want to learn openly, share my journey honestly, and hopefully encourage other women to start their own.

This blog will document everything: my career in technology, learning AI, building projects, overcoming self-doubt, and exploring ways to create meaningful impact for women.

I don't yet know the perfect path. It might be through mentoring, volunteering, creating educational content, building AI tools, supporting nonprofits, or starting community initiatives. I don't have every answer today, and that's okay.

Every meaningful mission begins with one small step. If my journey helps even one woman believe in herself, speak up, learn a new skill, or become financially independent, then sharing it will be worth it.

This Is My Commitment

To keep learning, keep building, and use whatever knowledge I gain to create opportunities for other women.

I'm finding my voice again.

And this is just the beginning.
